Monastery News & Bulletins

Official announcements, development movement milestones, and spiritual programs at Island Hermitage.

15
NOV
Development Movement

Phase II Restoration of Historic Forest Kutis Commences

Under the guidance of the resident Maha Sangha, preservation artisans have begun stabilizing century-old earthen walls and clay roofs on Metiduwa island to ensure peaceful shelter for recluses.

Restoration • Monastic Dwellings
28
NOV
Spiritual Observance

Full Moon Poya Day Meditation & Silent Reflection

Devotees and upasakas are invited to observe the Eight Precepts (Uposatha Sila) and participate in guided meditation discourses at the main shrine pavilion from 6:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Poya • Vipassana • Sila
05
DEC
Annual Ceremony

Annual Katina Chivara Robe Offering Ceremony

Marking the completion of the three-month Vassana rains retreat by the resident Sangha, the sacred Katina Robe offering will be conducted with deep devotion by the Dodanduwa community.

Katina • Vassana • Merits
12
DEC
Library Conservation

Digitization of Rare Palm-Leaf and German Manuscripts

A specialized archival team has initiated the high-resolution digital scanning of rare Pali ola leaf manuscripts and personal letters of Ven. Nyanatiloka and Ven. Nyanaponika for global scholarly access.

Archives • Tipitaka • Research
20
JAN
Environmental Protection

Ratgama Lake Mangrove Sanctuary Conservation Drive

In partnership with local conservationists, tree planting of native mangrove saplings and freshwater wetland protection around Polgasduwa and Metiduwa has been successfully inaugurated.

Ecology • Lake Sanctuary • Mangroves
09
JUL
Historical Commemoration

115th Anniversary of Island Hermitage Foundation

Commemorating July 9, 1911, when Most Ven. German Nyanatiloka Mahathera first set foot on Polgasduwa, an all-night Paritta chanting ceremony and Sanghika Dana will be held in tribute.

115 Years • Heritage • Mahathera
