Pilgrimage coordination, dana offering guidelines, and inquiries for Island Hermitage Dodanduwa.

Visiting Instructions & Monastic Sanctity

Preserving Noble Silence

Polgasduwa is a sacred contemplative sanctuary. All visitors and pilgrims are kindly asked to preserve silence, dress modestly in light/white clothes, turn off mobile ringers, and refrain from wandering into monks' private kutis without permission.

Daily Timings

Morning Dana: 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM
Afternoon Visiting: 01:30 PM – 04:30 PM

Boat Ferry Service

Board the monastery boat at the Dodanduwa mainland jetty on Ratgama Lake. Prior notice is appreciated for dana bookings and research appointments.

Offering Alms (Dana)

Devotees who wish to offer breakfast (heeldana) or lunch (divaldana) to the resident Sangha are requested to register the date in advance.

Geographical Coordinates

Island Coordinates: 6°06'35" N, 80°08'40" E
Lake: Ratgama Lagoon (Freshwater wetland sanctuary)
District: Galle District, Southern Province, Sri Lanka

Distance: 12 km north of Galle Fort, 6 km south of Hikkaduwa.
