Repository of Wisdom

A Century of Preserving the True Dhamma

The library at Polgasduwa Island Hermitage was established simultaneously with the hermitage in 1911 by Ven. Nyanatiloka. It houses thousands of rare volumes in Pali, Sinhala, German, English, French, and Burmese scripts, as well as an irreplaceable archive of traditional Sri Lankan palm-leaf (ola) manuscripts.

It was at these very study desks that iconic translators such as Ven. Nyanatiloka, Ven. Nyanaponika, and Ven. Ñāṇamoli painstakingly translated the Tripitaka and Visuddhimagga, illuminating the path of meditation for millions worldwide.

Library Digitization & Preservation Movement

As part of the active Developing Movement at Polgasduwa, an initiative is underway to digitize deteriorating century-old paper volumes, palm leaf bundles, and handwritten letters exchanged between Ven. Nyanatiloka, Ven. Nyanaponika, and European scholars. Scholars and monastics who wish to consult manuscripts for academic or Dhamma research may schedule an appointment via our Contact Page.
